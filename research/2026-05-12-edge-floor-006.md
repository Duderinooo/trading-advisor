# 2026-05-12 — Edge floor bumped 0.04 → 0.06 (fee-drag adjustment)

## Symptom

After 6 weeks live: bot's average trade was net-zero after Trade Republic fees
(€2 roundtrip on €80–100 positions = 2–2.5% fee-drag per trade). Setups with
`edge=0.04` passed the gate but produced expectancy ≈ €0 after fees.

R-Multiple stats showed loser-trades larger than winner-trades on average (0.81
ratio). Indicated that low-edge setups didn't have enough buffer to absorb
slippage + fees.

## Root cause

`MIN_EXPECTED_EDGE = 0.04` (a kelly-positive threshold) doesn't account for
fixed-fee drag. At low edge, even a winning thesis becomes break-even after
costs.

## Fix

Bump floor:
```python
MIN_EXPECTED_EDGE = 0.06  # was 0.04
```

Plus complementary tightening (same day):
- `MIN_RS_20D_VS_INDEX_PCT`: -1.0 → 0.0 (strict outperformer-only)
- `MIN_CONFLUENCE_SCORE`: 5 → 6
- `MAX_POSITION_SIZE_PERCENT`: 30 → 20 (smaller position-cap, faster sample
  accumulation, less emotional impact per loss)

## Constants

| File | Constant | Old | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| `config/risk.py` | `MIN_EXPECTED_EDGE` | 0.04 | 0.06 |
| `config/execution.py` | `MIN_RS_20D_VS_INDEX_PCT` | -1.0 | 0.0 |
| `config/execution.py` | `MIN_CONFLUENCE_SCORE` | 5 | 6 |
| `config/risk.py` | `MAX_POSITION_SIZE_PERCENT` | 30.0 | 20.0 |

## Lessons

- **Edge-floor must include fee-drag.** Don't set edge thresholds on
  pre-fee math when fixed fees > 1% of position.
- **Quality > quantity at small capital.** Fewer-but-higher-conviction beats
  many-thin-edge under fee-drag.
- **Multiple thresholds tightened together** because they all feed the same
  decision: edge × confluence × RS = setup quality. Loosening one undoes the
  others.
